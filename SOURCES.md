# Research sources

## Starting points

For the royal promise, begin with 2 Samuel 7:12–16 and Jeremiah 23:5–6. For the earliest claim about Jesus, read 1 Corinthians 15:3–11 alongside Galatians 1:13–20 and Romans 1:3–4. For the public-restoration objection, read Isaiah 2:2–4 and Ezekiel 37:21–28 alongside Paul’s description of the reign in 1 Corinthians 15:20–28.

The bibliography below brings together the source lists prepared for the academic paper and the amended debate. The slide transcript gives passage links, image credits and sources at the relevant slide.
